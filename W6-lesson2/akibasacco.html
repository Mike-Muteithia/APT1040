Welcome to Akiba SACCO

Savings & Memberships Terms


Share Capital
Permanent, non-refundable capital that defines your ownership in the SACCO. Earns dividends annually.
Monthly Deposits
Refundable savings used as collateral for loans. Must be contributed monthly (Minimum KES 1,000).
Benevolent Fund
A mandatory monthly contribution (KES 100) to assist members in times of bereavement.
